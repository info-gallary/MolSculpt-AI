    Gesture guide

    ☝️

    Point

    Move the cursor. Keep your hand roughly 40–70 cm from the camera.

    👌

    Pinch

    Touch thumb and index finger to click: place an atom, grow a chain, change a bond, press a button.

    🤏

    Pinch & drag

    From an atom, drag to draw a bond. It snaps to 30° angles and to nearby atoms.

    🖐️

    Hold three fingers

    Index, middle and ring finger up: opens a radial element menu at the cursor. Move into a slice, then pinch or dwell.

    ✋

    Open palm

    The rest pose. Nothing is triggered, so you can relax your hand at any time.

    ✌️

    Hold victory

    Undo the last action. Hold again to keep undoing.

    👍

    Hold thumbs-up

    Ask the AI chemist to characterise the molecule.

    ✊

    Fist

    Grab and pan the whole canvas while the fist is closed.

    ⏱️

    Dwell

    Hover over any button for about a second to press it without pinching.

    Keyboard & voice

    Keys: C N O S P F H · L (Cl) · B (benzene) · 1/2/3 bond · D/M/E/Q tools · Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y · Enter analyse · Shift+F fit · right-click opens the element menu. Voice (Chrome/Edge): say “carbon”, “double bond”, “benzene”, “erase”, “undo”, “analyse”, “read report”.

    Privacy: video frames are processed on this device by MediaPipe and are never displayed, stored or uploaded. Voice recognition uses the browser's speech service.
